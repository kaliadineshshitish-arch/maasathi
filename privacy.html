Privacy Policy — Maasathi

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · App version 1.0

Sabse zaroori baat: Maasathi aapka data aapke phone mein hi rakhti hai. Hum koi server par aapki pregnancy ki jaankari save nahi karte, na kisi ko bechte ya share karte hain.

Yeh Privacy Policy batati hai ki Maasathi ("App", "hum") aapki personal jaankari kaise handle karti hai, aur aapko Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") ke tahat milne wale adhikaaron ke baare mein jaankari deti hai. Yeh App Dinesh Kalia (Maasathi) ("Data Fiduciary") dwara chalayi jaati hai.

1. Hum kaunsi jaankari lete hain

  • Aap khud jo bharti hain: naam (optional), due date / LMP, symptoms, weight, BP, mood, appointments, notes, checklist.
  • Kuch nahi: hum aapki location, contacts, camera, ya kisi doosri app ka data nahi lete.
  • Koi tracking nahi: koi analytics, koi advertising ID, koi third-party SDK nahi.

2. Data kahan rehta hai

Saara data sirf aapke device ke local storage mein rehta hai. App internet se connect nahi karti (offline-first design). Agar aap App lock PIN set karti hain, to data AES-GCM 256-bit encryption se surakshit hota hai — key aapke PIN se banti hai, jo humein pata nahi hoti.

Payment (Premium) ke liye ek exception: agar aap Premium kharidti hain, to hum apne server (Cloudflare) par sirf itna rakhte hain — ek random device ID, aapka plan, aur premium ki valid date. Aapki pregnancy ki koi jaankari server par kabhi nahi jaati.

3. Data ka uddeshya (Purpose Limitation)

Aapka data sirf aapko tracker, reminders aur summary dikhane ke liye use hota hai. Kisi aur kaam ke liye nahi.

4. Kya hum data share karte hain

Aapki pregnancy ki jaankari: bilkul nahi. Hum aapka health data kisi third party, advertiser, ya data broker ko nahi bechte ya share karte hain. Koi ad network integrated nahi hai.

Payment (Premium): Agar aap Premium kharidti hain, to payment Cashfree Payments (ek RBI-regulated payment gateway) se process hoti hai. Us waqt hum Cashfree ko sirf zaroori order jaankari dete hain — jaise order ID, amount, aur aapka phone/email (agar aap dein). Hum aapka card, UPI ya bank detail kabhi store nahi karte — wo seedha Cashfree ke paas, unke surakshit system mein rehta hai. Cashfree ki apni privacy policy bhi lagu hoti hai.

5. Aapke adhikaar (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to Access: Settings → "Mera data download karein" se poora data JSON mein le sakti hain.
  • Right to Erasure: Settings → "Poora data delete karein" se sab kuch turant delete kar sakti hain.
  • Right to Correction: Kisi bhi entry ko edit / delete kar sakti hain.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: App uninstall karke ya data delete karke consent wapas le sakti hain.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: neeche diye Grievance Officer se sampark karein.

6. Consent

App pehli baar kholte waqt aapse spasht sehmati leti hai. Sehmati ke bina app use nahi hoti. Sehmati ka record aapke device par timestamp ke saath rakha jaata hai.

7. Bachchon ka data

Yeh App 18 saal se kam umar ke users ke liye nahi hai. Hum bachchon ka data jaan-boojh kar collect nahi karte.

8. Data suraksha

Local-only storage, optional encryption, no network transmission, aur Content Security Policy jaisi technical suraksha. Phir bhi, koi bhi system 100% surakshit nahi hota — apne device ka PIN/lock zaroor rakhein.

9. Data breach

Kyunki data aapke device se bahar nahi jaata, central breach ka risk bahut kam hai. Phir bhi agar koi aisi ghatna ho jo aapko prabhavit kare, hum DPDP Act ke tahat zaroori suchna aur Data Protection Board ko report karenge. Contact: kaliadineshshitish@gmail.com
